# The words to give your AI

Copy everything in the box into a chat with your own AI (Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, any of them). It will ask what you need to decide, then write your cards. Paste what it writes into the page's **Use it with your own AI** box and press **Show my cards**.

```text
Help me settle my open decisions with the yes not yes.

1. If you do not already know them, ask me which decisions are open.
2. Write one card for each, seven at most, as a JSON list in one code block, and nothing else:
[{"id": "a1", "t": "a title of three or four words", "about": ["what this is about, in plain words, from what I have told you", "a second short paragraph if it helps"], "q": "one question, asked the positive way round: Do you want …?", "yes": "exactly what happens if I tap Yes", "alt": "your best guess at what I want instead", "alt2": "a second, different alternative"}]
   Context on every card, before the question. Use what I told you, not your own view, and do not steer.
3. I will paste it into the yes not yes page, tap my answers, and paste them back to you.
4. Read my answers like this. YES: settled. YES, ON A CONDITION: only with the condition under it. NOT YES. Instead: settled that way. Left open: leave it alone. NOT YES with no tap on an alternative: I have not chosen, so ask me what I am driving at. EXPLAIN THIS MORE: give me that card again with more context. My own words outrank my taps. Never ask again what a tap has settled. If you reword a question, give it a new id.
```

The same words are built into the example page, with a Copy button.
